HOW TO PLAY

Complete all four rooms to escape.

  • Each room has its own challenge.
  • Your timer starts when you enter a room.
  • Your time stops when you complete the challenge.
  • You can complete the rooms in any order.
  • Your overall time is the sum of all four room times.
